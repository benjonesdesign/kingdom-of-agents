    HOW IT WORKS

    THE MAP

    Every project is a town and every lane an adventurer building it. Choose a place to travel there. Couriers on the roads carry letters between towns; the King's herald in purple and gold carries your decrees.

    HOW LANES REPORT IN

    Each lane keeps one record up to date. Buildings rise with progress, the villager acts out state, waitingOn sends them to the lane they need, talkingTo sends them down the road to another product's town, and each entry in helpers (a lane's running subagents) appears as an apprentice. Lanes that go quiet for two hours head to the tavern.
